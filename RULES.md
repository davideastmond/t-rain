# How to play T-rain

A broad overview for new players. The full rulebook is SPEC.md — this is
the skim-before-your-first-game version, and the copy for the in-app
rules page.

## The goal

Race your pieces across the board. First player to get **5 of their 15
pieces** onto the far row wins.

## The board

30×31 tiles, with a neutral row in the middle. Each game gets one biome
theme (grass, desert, or ice) — cosmetic only. Terrain matters:

- **Plains** — costs 1 point to enter
- **Forest** — costs 2 points to enter
- **Water** — impassable

## Setup (120 seconds)

Place your 15 pieces in your back 3 rows, and hide **10 traps** in your
own half plus the neutral row (at most 3 on the neutral row). Run out of
time and the rest auto-place.

## Your turn

You get a shared pool of **10 movement points** per turn, split across
any of your pieces — but no piece may spend more than **3 points** in a
turn. Pieces move up/down/left/right only (no diagonals), can't share a
tile, and can't pass through other pieces. Unspent points don't carry
over.

## Traps

Invisible to your opponent. The instant an enemy piece steps on one, it's
eliminated and the trap is revealed and spent — even mid-turn, and even
on the goal row: the trap resolves before scoring.

## Scoring & nerfs

Each piece that crosses earns you a **nerf token**, usable from your next
turn (hold at most 2). Spend **1 token + 3 movement points** to eliminate
an enemy piece that's next to — orthogonally adjacent to — one of yours.
No trap check, no moving required.

## Attrition

If you ever drop below 5 pieces able to score, you lose on the spot —
no matter the score.

## Playing online

Create a game from the home page and share the invite link. Your
opponent joins, both players set up, and turns sync automatically.
First player is random.
